## ✨ About SkillBridge

SkillBridge brings student skill development and career guidance together in one place. It is designed to help learners discover skills, connect with mentors, share what they know, and explore marketplace opportunities—all while keeping their learning journey organized through a student dashboard.

The idea is simple: make it easier for students to move from **learning** to **doing**, and from **doing** to **growing**.

## 🎯 The challenge—and the idea

Students often want to build practical skills and prepare for a career, but can struggle to find direction, get useful guidance, or put their abilities to work. Learning can feel disconnected from real opportunities.

SkillBridge aims to connect those steps: discover a skill, learn with guidance, practice and share it, and find people or opportunities that can help you progress.

## 🧩 What the platform offers

| Area | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| 📚 **Learn skills** | Explore skill development as part of a student’s growth journey. |
| 🧑‍🏫 **Find mentors** | Seek guidance and learn from people with relevant experience. |
| 🎓 **Teach skills** | Share knowledge and help other learners develop. |
| 🛍️ **Marketplace** | Connect skills with people and opportunities that need them. |
| 📊 **Student dashboard** | Keep the student experience and progress journey organized in one place. |

## 🔄 A learner’s journey

**Discover → Learn → Get guidance → Teach or apply → Find opportunities → Grow**

Students can use SkillBridge to focus on learning, seek mentorship when they need direction, share skills with peers, and explore marketplace opportunities. The dashboard provides a home for the student experience.
